The Problem
02 / 13
Enterprise AI has a sovereignty crisis.

Every major enterprise runs AI on shared infrastructure — cloud APIs, foreign model providers, multi-tenant data centres. This is now illegal in regulated industries and strategically indefensible everywhere else.

⚠ The regulatory burning platform
DORA Article 9 (Jan 2025): EU mandates crypto-agility across 21+ financial entity types. NIS2 explicit PQC (Jan 2026). CNSA 2.0 enforcement (Jan 2027). Fines up to 2% of global annual turnover. Non-compliance is not an option.

Nation-state actors are harvesting encrypted data today to decrypt with quantum computers in 5–10 years. Every financial record and state secret in transit is already compromised at the packet level. Migration to PQC is urgent, not eventual.
⚡ Cloud AI = data sovereignty violation
Routing sensitive workloads through OpenAI, Azure, or AWS violates GDPR data residency, DORA operational resilience, and NIS2 supply chain security simultaneously. Legal exposure is existential for regulated enterprises.

Competitors are bolting air-gap modes onto cloud-native architectures after the fact — expensive, brittle, unconvincing to regulators. McKinsey (Mar 2026): sovereign AI migrations take 3–4 years when built as an afterthought. First-mover window is closing.
