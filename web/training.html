🎯 The "Real Jobs" Principle

Every Level 2 and Level 3 module requires students to engage with real job postings and real clients. For students who cannot secure contracts immediately, the programme provides:

  • Mock contracts — Based on actual job postings from platforms like Upwork, Toptal, and Appen.
  • Simulated clients — Role-played by instructors with real-world scenarios.
  • Live project tracking — Students track their progress against real timelines and deliverables.

Pedagogical Framework

The curriculum is designed around four core pedagogical principles, drawn from academic research and industry best practice.

1. Competency-Based Education (CBE)

Holding learning outcomes constant while allowing time-to-completion to vary. The CBE-ADDIE Model integrates the well-established ADDIE framework with CBE principles, providing a robust approach for competency mapping, assessment design, content development, implementation, and continuous evaluation[reference:17][reference:18].

2. Skill Trees

Structuring educational content around skills — what a student is expected to be able to do. Skill Trees show dependencies between skills, coupled to Concept Trees that contain intuitive ideas. This framework has been shown to reduce student confusion and study time while improving learning outcomes[reference:19][reference:20].

3. Experiential Learning

Structuring each day around a key experiential module, delivering an active, high-energy, purpose-driven course that keeps students learning by doing[reference:21][reference:22]. This is the "learning by doing" approach that underpins the entire Rapid Phase.

4. EPA-Anchored Assessment

Entrustable Professional Activities (EPAs) are observable, measurable units of professional work that a supervisor can certify a trainee to perform unsupervised. Each level culminates in EPA-based assessments that validate real-world competence.
