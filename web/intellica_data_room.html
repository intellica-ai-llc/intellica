Valuation Methodology & Forward-Looking Statements

Each of the four product systems (Cortex, VeriCrypt, VeriChain, Verity) has been independently valued using three-method triangulation: (1) Comparable Company Analysis against publicly-traded and recently-transacted peers in each product's market segment; (2) Precedent Transaction Analysis drawing on disclosed M&A multiples in AI infrastructure, cybersecurity, Layer-1 blockchain, and core banking technology; (3) Discounted Cash Flow projections based on addressable market sizing and conservative penetration assumptions over a 7-year horizon. The Agent Seed Language (ASL) is not independently valued; its intellectual property value is subsumed within all four product valuations as the common platform substrate.

Comparable selection rationale per system: Cortex — Palantir AIP, ServiceNow, Cohere; VeriCrypt — Thales cybersecurity division, Wiz acquisition ($32B), CrowdStrike; VeriChain — Lighter capital opinion ($3B), EigenLayer, Celestia; Verity Banking — Thought Machine ($2.7B valuation), Fiserv, Temenos.
