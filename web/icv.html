The paradigm shift argument — stated precisely

The enterprise software industry ran on a single economic primitive for 25 years: the human seat. Every SAP, Oracle, Salesforce, and ServiceNow deployment is priced per user per month. In February 2026, the SaaSpocalypse wiped $2 trillion from enterprise software valuations in 48 hours — not from recession, but from the market repricing the realisation that AI agents are replacing human software operators at a ratio of roughly 1:5. The per-seat model is structurally broken.

Cortex was designed, years in advance, for the world that comes after. It is not a better enterprise app. It is the substrate that makes enterprise apps obsolete — invisibly, safely, and without triggering the status quo bias that kills every enterprise transformation programme. The absorption pipeline's core constraint (C10: 0% user detection rate) is not a UX nicety. It is the psychological mechanism by which paradigm shift actually happens in regulated enterprises.

VESSEL — the Verified Sovereign Execution Substrate — is the safety proof that no CIO or DPO has been able to articulate until now: a formally-verified, model-agnostic governance layer where no LLM ever sees raw enterprise data, no LLM ever triggers direct execution, and every action is cryptographically provenance-chained and regulator-replayable. This resolves the governance gap that is the primary reason 88% of enterprise AI stays in pilot purgatory.

The thesis in one sentence: Cortex is the sovereign infrastructure layer that absorbs every enterprise application invisibly while any current or future LLM operates safely inside VESSEL's formally-verified boundary — making it the last enterprise interface any organisation will ever need to procure.
